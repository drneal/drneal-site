KI-CAI Kenya Institute for Clinical AI →

A proposal for a permanent national institution that would train and certify the clinical AI competence of Kenya’s entire health workforce — doctors, clinical officers, nurses, pharmacists and laboratory staff alike.

The argument in one line: the variable that decides whether clinical AI helps or harms a patient is not the model, it is the training of the clinician in front of it. A 2025 randomised trial found that physicians who had already completed twenty hours of AI-literacy training still deferred to deliberately erroneous model output — so the obvious curriculum is the wrong curriculum.

Five professional tracks, five gated levels, assessment by simulation and workplace observation rather than attendance, and outcomes published whether or not they flatter us. The blueprint, competency standards, five-year financial model and evaluation design are published in full. Read the proposal →
